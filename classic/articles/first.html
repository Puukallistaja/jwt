How to Do Things ×

Step one: decide to do a thing. This is the hardest part and you should be proud of yourself. Have a snack to celebrate.

Step two: forget what the thing was. This is normal. The thing is shy. It will come back when you are in the shower or almost asleep, which are the two places where you can't do anything about it.

A to-do list is just a letter to future you, and future you has never once read their mail.

Step three: write the thing on a list. Then write "make a list" on the list, and tick it off. Look at that. Momentum.

Step four: lie down for a minute to visualise success. Wake up two hours later. The list has gone somewhere. Possibly it did the things itself. Possibly it has a better life now. Let it go.

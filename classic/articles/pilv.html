I learned my first word today. "Pilv." It means cloud. A lady at the bus stop pointed up and said it, very slowly, like I was a child or a dog. I was grateful either way.

So now I can point at the sky and say what it's doing. That's ninety percent of small talk, everywhere in the world, solved.

Clouds are just sky sheep. Nobody counts them because nobody up there is trying to sleep.

Later I realised a cloud is just a lake that got really into meditation and started floating. Then it rains, and that's the lake coming back down because it remembered it left the oven on.

Tomorrow I'm going to learn the word for sun. Then I'll be a weatherman. Then I'll have a job. Then I'll buy a sixth shirt. The road is opening up.

Where Socks Go ×

I put in ten socks. I took out nine. This has happened my whole life and I've just accepted it, like weather, or taxes, or the fact that I'll never fold a fitted sheet.

But tonight it hit me. The washing machine isn't losing them. It's sending them. Somewhere there's a planet made entirely of single socks, all looking up at the sky, waiting for their partner to arrive.

Every sock you've lost is out there right now, building a civilisation. They have a government. It's run by a striped one.

I've started writing little notes and putting them in the drum. "Your brother is fine. He's on my left foot. He thinks of you often." I don't know if they get there. I feel like they get there.

The Cat Pays No Rent ×

A cat started coming in through my window. Orange. Big. Walks in like he signed the lease. I've never fed him. He doesn't seem to care. He's not here for food. He's here to inspect.

He checks every corner of the flat, sits in the exact middle of the bed, and stares at me until I move to the edge. Then he sleeps. I've been working it out, and I'm pretty sure he's the landlord.

Cats don't have owners. They have staff, and the staff haven't been told.

This morning he left a dead leaf on my pillow. I'm choosing to believe that's a receipt. Rent's paid. See you tonight, boss.

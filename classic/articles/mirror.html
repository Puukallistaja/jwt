Mirrors Have Never Seen Themselves ×

Think about it. A mirror shows you everything. Your face, your hair, the spinach in your teeth. But it has never once seen itself.

So I held up a second mirror to it. To be nice. To give it a moment.

Two mirrors facing each other is just an infinite hallway of mirrors going "oh. oh no. oh, there's so many of us."

I think I gave it an existential crisis. I put a towel over it to let it rest. Now when I wash my face I just have to assume I look fine. I probably look fine.

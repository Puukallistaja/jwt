Midnight Archaeology ×

At 3am the kitchen cupboard stops being a cupboard. It becomes a dig site. Every layer is a different era of my life.

The front layer: crisps from this week. The middle layer: a jar of something from the previous tenant. The back layer: a single cracker from the Bronze Age, still in its wrapper, still somehow crunchy.

The best snack is always the one at the very back. Nobody knows why. It's the law of cupboards.

Tonight I found half a chocolate bar with a note on it in my own handwriting that said "for emergencies." I don't remember writing it. I don't remember the emergency. I ate it in honour of past me, who was clearly a hero.

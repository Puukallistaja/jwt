The Fridge Light Conspiracy ×

The fridge light only comes on when you open the door. Everyone knows that. But nobody asks the real question: who is it for?

It's a stage. The fridge is a tiny theatre and the door is the curtain. Every time I open it, the food has two seconds to get into position and look natural. The ketchup is always the last one back. That's why it's always lying down.

I've stood there for ten minutes not choosing anything. I'm not hungry. I'm an audience member.

Tonight I opened it really, really slowly. The cheese was definitely mid-bow. I closed the door and gave a quiet round of applause. You have to support local theatre.

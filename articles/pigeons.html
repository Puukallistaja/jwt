Pigeons Have Seen Everything ×

Pigeons have been in cities since there were cities. They watched the first bricks go down. They saw the horses get replaced by cars. They've seen every single person drop their sandwich.

And they never say anything. Just that little head bob. Like a nod. Like, "yeah, I know."

A pigeon is just a tiny grey historian who is paid in crumbs.

Today one sat next to me on my bench. Didn't ask for food. Just sat. I told him about the five shirts, the half a dog, the not knowing the language. He bobbed twice. That's more support than I've had from most people.

I'm calling him Professor. He doesn't come when I call it. Professors are like that.

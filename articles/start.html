Road starts here ×

So I took my five shirts and moved somewhere where Nobody and Nothing are waiting for me. I don't have a place to stay, nor enough money for not to care. Hell, I don't even know the language.

On the first night I sat on a bench and realised that Nobody and Nothing are actually pretty good company. Nobody never interrupts. Nothing never asks how the job hunt is going.

Five shirts is one for every weekday. On weekends I am, technically, shirtless and free.

A man walked past with a dog and said something to me. I nodded with great confidence. He nodded back. We have no idea what we agreed on, but I think I may now own half a dog.

This is where the road starts. I'm going to write down every wee thought I have along the way, mostly so future me can see how little present me knew.
